#1. House Saison

ABV: 7.25%

IBU: 16

Base Malts (~82%): 2-Row

Adjuncts (~18%): Flaked Oats, Red X, Chit

Hops: Simcoe, Hallertau Blanc

Yeast Strain: Jasper's Poperinge Saison

Tasting Notes: Here we go again; same yeast strain, different malt/hops. Crisp with a sweetness that leads to the phenolic funk from the yeast. The Hallertau Blanc give a grassiness/white grape essence without being overly earthy. Balanced and Complex.

Tastes Like: Firestone Walker Opal, Boulevard Tank 7

#2. Sower's Saison

ABV: 5.9%

IBU: 18

Base Malts (~97%): 2-Row

Adjuncts (~3%): Chit

Hops: Columbus, Mt. Hood

Yeast Strain: Jasper's Poperinge Saison

Tasting Notes: Vibrant, crisp and bright. Simple base malts and hop profile provide the stage for the Saison Yeast strain to flourish and come to life. Great start to the gardener's season of sowing.

Tastes Like: EVO Sprung Ale, Firestone Walker Opal (but lighter)
