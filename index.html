#1. House Saison

ABV: 7.25%

IBU: 16

Base Malts (~82%): 2-Row

Adjuncts (~18%): Flaked Oats, Red X, Chit

Hops: Simcoe, Hallertau Blanc

Yeast Strain: Jasper's Poperinge Saison

Tasting Notes: Crisp and bright sweetness, balanced by a mix of bitterness and white grape. Blending into a set of tropical and stone fruit notes that balances the phenolic clove character. The finish has a sweet banana, bittering citrus taste with a slight edge of burn. More like the good old days.

Tastes Like: Firestone Walker Opal, Boulevard Tank 7
