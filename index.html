#2. West Coasty IPA

ABV: 7.6%

IBU: 62

Base Malts (~65%): 2-Row

Adjuncts (~35%): Caramunich (60L), Melanoidin, Chit

Hops: Simcoe, Centennial, Chinook

Yeast Strain: OYL-004 (Chico) & Nottingham's

Tasting Notes: Pine and Resin meet the caramel and malty backbone to deliver a traditional West Coast IPA experience. Darker in color, leaning into autumn, this IPA brings nostalgia with its harkening back to when West Coast was amber, pine and delicious.

Tastes Like: Sierra Nevada Pale Ale, Bell's Two Hearted Ale
