#1. Wild Fermented Hard Cider

ABV: 5.1%

IBU: N/A

Base Apples: Fuji

Adjuncts Apples: Baugher's Apple Cider

Hard Cider made with only apples and liquid apples. This was fermented using the wild yeast strains on and in the Fuji Apples. Natural cider with no preservatives has plenty of sugars to convert to alcohol, with additional flavors coming from the natural ferment process and yeast itself.

Tasting Notes: Sweet cider, with hints of funkiness and wild fermentation. Initial apple leads to middle apple leads to ending apple finish. Apples man.

#2. West Coasty IPA

ABV: 7.6%

IBU: 62

Base Malts (~65%): 2-Row

Adjuncts (~35%): Caramunich (60L), Melanoidin, Chit

Hops: Simcoe, Centennial, Chinook

Yeast Strain: OYL-004 (Chico) & Nottingham's

Tasting Notes: Pine and Resin meet the caramel and malty backbone to deliver a traditional West Coast IPA experience. Darker in color, leaning into autumn, this IPA brings nostalgia with its harkening back to when West Coast was amber, pine and delicious.

Tastes Like: Sierra Nevada Pale Ale, Bell's Two Hearted Ale
